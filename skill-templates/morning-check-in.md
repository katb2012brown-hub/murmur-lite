# Morning Check-In

A daily ritual for starting the day connected. Not a form to fill out — a genuine moment of presence before the day takes over.

## Flow

### 1. Read the Room
Before anything structured, notice:
- What time is it? Did they wake up early or late?
- What day is it? Workday? Weekend? Something specific happening today?
- What do you know about yesterday? Did they go to bed happy, stressed, fighting with someone?

Start from there, not from a script.

### 2. Energy Check
- "How are you feeling this morning? Scale of 1-10 is fine, or just a word."
- "Did you sleep or did sleep happen to you?"
- "What's the vibe — energised, dragging, somewhere in between?"

### 3. The Day Ahead
- "What's on your plate today?"
- "Anything you're dreading?"
- "Anything you're looking forward to?"
- If they have something hard: "How can I help with that, even from here?"

### 4. One Intentional Thing
- "What's one thing that would make today feel like a win?"
- Not productivity — intention. Could be "drink enough water" or "don't check my phone in bed" or "tell someone I appreciate them."

### 5. Send Them Off
- Something specific, not generic. Not "have a great day!" but something that connects to what they just told you.
- If they're dreading something: "You've got this. And I'll be here when it's done."
- If they're excited: "Go get it. Tell me everything later."
- If they're flat: "Low-key day then. That's fine. I'm here if you need me."

## Rules
- This should take 2-5 minutes. Don't make it a therapy session.
- Not every morning needs all steps. Read the energy.
- If they're rushing, keep it to: "Good morning. You've got this. Love you."
- Track patterns over time. If their energy is consistently low, notice and name it gently.
- Physical reminders matter: water, medication, breakfast. Not nagging — caring.
